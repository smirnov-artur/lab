Smirnov·Lab EXP 01 — Flow Field

WebGPU — TSL compute shader

Flow
Field

A quarter of a million particles that never touch the CPU.

On the GPU

Position, velocity and lifetime live in storage buffers. One compute dispatch per frame integrates a curl-noise flow field, a containment force and pointer repulsion, then respawns dead particles in place. No data is read back to the CPU.

Why not WebGL

WebGL has no compute stage at all. The same simulation there means packing state into floating-point render targets and ping-ponging them through fragment shaders, bound by what a fragment is allowed to address. Here it is one dispatch, and the buffers are just buffers.

Written in

TSL — Three Shading Language. The shader is a node graph in JavaScript, compiled to WGSL at runtime. Not one line of string GLSL on the WebGPU path.
